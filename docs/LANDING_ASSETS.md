Prompt final:

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single polished editorial raster illustration for ReptileApp, a Spanish reptile care record app. One charming but anatomically plausible small gecko, full body, three-quarter overhead view, large curved tail forming an elegant open C, head looking curiously up and slightly left, toes fully visible. Mature contemporary nature editorial illustration, rich hand-painted gouache with subtle fine paper texture inside the animal, sculptural clean silhouette, refined shapes and warm personality, not childish cartoon or app icon. Palette: bright yellow lime #d7f15b on back, deep forest petroleum #153d32 for shadows and softly spotted pattern, small coral accents only on toe pads. A friendly eye, closed mouth. Transparent background with genuine alpha, no ground, no rectangle, no text, no letters, no UI, no objects, no drop shadow, no neon glow. Center full gecko with generous transparent margin, all tail and feet inside frame. Intended as a large decorative cutout on a dark green web hero.
